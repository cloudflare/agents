Support Pi 1.5 (`~1.5.0`) and take `@earendil-works/pi-schema` as an optional peer. `PiSession.events()` is now `watch()`, and `retry()` and the `parked` status are removed because Pi ends restart-limited tasks. One-way: Pi 1.5 migrates stored `pi.live` and task documents, which Pi 1.4 cannot read. See [the Pi harness docs](https://github.com/cloudflare/agents/blob/main/docs/agents/harnesses/pi.md).
