`agents/lifecycle` is no longer experimental, and neither are the `Scheduler` (`agents/schedules`), `State` (`agents/state`), `WebSockets` (`agents/websockets`) and `MCPClientManager` (`agents/mcp/client`) capabilities. Their `@experimental` tags are removed. The Cap'n Web WebSocket transport, `Queue`, `Tasks`, `Streams`, `Sessions`, `RoutedAgents` and `Browser` stay experimental. See [Lifecycle](https://github.com/cloudflare/agents/blob/main/docs/agents/lifecycle.md).
